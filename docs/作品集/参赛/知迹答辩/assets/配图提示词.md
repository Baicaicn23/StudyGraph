### English (prefix)

```
Bright high-key photorealistic 3D render, soft natural top light from the upper right at
45 degrees, gentle shadows in a consistent direction, clean cream and ivory background,
matte materials (frosted ceramic, matte paper, pale wood, matte resin), 85mm lens look,
shallow depth of field with gently blurred background, subject centered or offset to one side
with generous negative space, professional academic presentation illustration.
No dark background, no mirror reflections, no neon, no rainbow gradients, no cartoon,
no flat vector illustration, no people, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark.
```

### 三条硬约束

1. **图上不要写字** —— 豆包/Seedream 4.0 中文渲染虽准，但图上带字会与 PPT 文本重影。
2. **不要人物** —— AI 人脸是答辩事故高发区。
3. **两张图必须像同一个场景** —— 同一张书桌、同一套光线与材质，只是物件不同。
   p16 是 p01 的"事情做完了"版本。
